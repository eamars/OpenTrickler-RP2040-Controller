## What we want

ADR-009 is two steps, in this order:
1. **The calibration:** the user runs it for the unit and for each powder, from the screen or the web. It measures the cup, the scale, and each tube's least speed for the powder, and keeps them.
2. **The tuning:** the predictor and the PID auto-tuner, made from what step 1 keeps.

Step 2 depends on step 1: it takes step 1's records as its input, and has none without them. It starts only once step 1 is fully complete: a user can run the calibration from the 12864 and from the web on a unit, its records are kept and backed up, and nothing of it is left open. ADR-009 is these two steps and nothing more.

- Step 2 starts only once the calibration is fully complete, the screen and the web included.

- The fine threshold is the user's expectation, the tolerance of a throw. The calibration never writes it.
- The cup comes first. The user gives its capacity by filling it with the knob; a pour that would pass it pauses the calibration.

## Step 1: the calibration

### What it records

| # | What | Kept in | How it is found |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The cup's capacity | the charge settings, appended | The user trickles with the knob until the cup holds as much as they want it to; the reading, shown and confirmed, is the capacity. | 2 | The empty cup's weight | the charge settings, appended | The cup lifted off the zeroed scale: the reading falls by its weight, so a lifted cup can be told from a light reading. **No longer measured** (the owner, 2026-11-27, the review's O1): nothing read it, so the cup's part fills straight after the zero; the field stays in the record, unused |
| 3 | The scale's settle time | the scale settings, appended | How long a small change takes from the reading's first move to its rest: when it first shows its final value and never again strays more than one step from it. | 4 | The scale's lag | the scale settings, appended | How far the reading runs behind the powder poured, in seconds, by flow rate: over a steady run and its stop, the powder poured but not yet shown, divided by the powder poured. A table of five flows: about 0.2, 0.4 and 0.8 gr/s on the fine tube, 3 and 10 on the coarse |
| 5 | Each tube's least speed | the powder profile's own field (its minimum speed) | Twice the slowest speed at which the tube pours |
| 6 | The fine tube's weight a turn | the powder profile, `fine_weight_per_turn`, appended to each profile with `coarse_weight_per_turn` after it; `compatibility` kept where it was (`src/profile.h`). (First built in `compatibility`'s place; moved to the end on 2026-11-26, never insert) | The prime's (item 5): its grains a turn where it streams. Kept since 2026-11-26 as step 2's first figure for the powder: without it a profile fast enough to finish before the coarse tube's quiet never teaches one (progress, 2026-11-26) |

**The least speed** (item 5), each tube judged on its own. The tube is first turned at the middle of its motor's limits in ratio until two runs pour alike (the prime), which gives its grains a turn where it streams; then a ladder of speeds from the motor's least up to the prime's speed, no faster:
- it is twice the slowest speed at which the tube pours: that speed's runs together pour at least half the tube's grains a turn at the prime's speed. A confident value, the measured minimum doubled. The runs are judged by their sum, as one slow run is a few kernels, and at each speed the run farthest from the speed's median is left out as a clump or a lull;
- a tube whose speeds give no least leaves it as it was, and says so.

On the bench (2026-11-25) this gives the fine tube 0.020 rev/s in each of three runs (it pours at its motor's least, 0.010), the coarse tube 0.16 rev/s. The tubes register ever more alike with speed, kernel by kernel to a stream, with no knee, so the least is not judged by how alike the runs are (progress, 2026-11-25, 01:57).

Item 5 belongs to this powder on this unit's tubes and scale, not to the powder alone. The coarse and fine stop thresholds, each profile's maximum speeds and the PID stay as the user has them; today's loop charges as it does now.

### To settle before approval

1. **Appending without a reset** (approved with gate 2 and built, 2026-11-25): today's loader would reset the charge settings and the scale settings (the scale's make and baud rate with them) on the update that appends items 1 to 4. Proposed: a record whose CRC32 fails at the struct's size is tried at each shorter size, from one byte less down to one, until a CRC32 matches; that size is the previous version's. Its bytes are kept, the appended fields take their defaults, and it is written once the update is kept, as defaults are today. It holds when:
   - fields are only appended at the end of a flat stored struct (never inside an array's element, such as one powder profile of eight);
   - the struct grows by them: `sizeof` checked before and after, since a field that fits in the old struct's tail padding leaves the size as it was and reads that padding;
   - a record that matches at no size is taken as damaged and gets the defaults, as today. A wrong match is a chance of one in about four billion for each size tried.

   Shown by an update from today's release that keeps every setting.

### How the unit shows it works

No throw's result is judged.
- A user runs the calibration on a unit from the 12864 and from the web, from nothing to its records, each of its steps shown, and can stop it.
- Each part runs alone and in any order: a powder calibrated on a unit whose scale and cup were never calibrated runs on their defaults; a scale calibrated again leaves every powder's record as it was; each item can be run by itself.
- No pour passes the cup's capacity: one that would pauses the calibration and asks for the cup to be emptied.
- Every item of the table is recorded, kept across a restart, held in the backup, and given back by a restore; an update keeps every setting the user had.
- The values it records are those its runs' recordings give, checked on the PC.
- A step made to fail (no reading from the scale, a tube that pours nothing, the cup lifted mid-run) says what failed, retries nothing, and leaves its item at its default value.
- Its time and the powder it poured are reported, not judged.
- A charge then throws with them.

### What it does

**The charge controller, a module.** Its core is plain C with nothing of the RTOS, the motors or the scale in it, as step 1's method (`calibration_method.c`); the charge mode drives it and the simulation runs the same C.
- **What it is given:** the target and the user's tolerance; the profile's gains and speed limits; step 1's records (the lag by flow, the settle time); and its channels, each a feed with its own rate (a tube's in turns a second), its least and most rate, and the stop point it works to (the coarse's, the fine's).
- **What it is fed:** each reading with its time, and the rate each channel was last set to.
- **What it gives:** each channel's rate, when each stops, and at the throw's end its record (below).
- **What stays outside it, in the charge mode:** the throw's states (the zero, the cup off and back), a missing reading's pause, a driver's fault, the verdict's wait, the events and the lights. A design other than tubes replaces the channels and keeps the rest.

**The coarse tube** is today's: its PID on the reading's error to the target less its threshold, stopped once the reading reaches it.

**The predictor** serves the fine tube alone, below the coarse tube's threshold, where the throw's last grain or gram is decided. Each reading, the forecast is the reading plus the fine tube's turns turned out and not yet shown, in 10 ms parcels of its rate, each due after the lag of the flow it was turned out at (step 1's table, straight between its points and the end's value beyond them), times its weight a turn. The weight a turn is carried from throw to throw in RAM, nothing stored: the median of the last five throws' own, each taken once the throw is at rest, from its verdict's reading (the fine tube's powder since the coarse tube's powder went quiet, over its turns not yet shown then). With none carried (a start, a change of powder), the forecast starts from step 1's own (item 6), until the throws give theirs. A powder never calibrated has none: its first throw takes the throw's own once a few tenths of a turn are shown (the slope of the reading on the fine turns shown, solved with the lags at its own flow), and before that the reading alone, as today; on a profile that finishes before the coarse tube's quiet it never gets one (2026-11-26). A powder's change of lot, a cold tube or a scale in grams is met within five throws. The fine tube's PID works on the forecast's error, and the throw is stopped by the reading alone, by main's rule: once the reading is less than the fine threshold under the target (the owner, 2026-11-26: "Legit mistake, must fix"; built first at the target itself, which left only the band's upper half). Never cut on the forecast (the owner, 2026-11-26, 09:45: "perhaps just don't cut the throw ... push all potential failure mode to overcharge"; 09:51: "Yes, build it"). Past the forecast's target the PID asks nothing and the fine tube creeps at its least until the reading is within the threshold: what the forecast held in the air lands, and a gap it had counted on is closed slowly instead of left short. The turns are counted from the rates set, not the step count, which has no sign.

**Its switch,** `forecast` in the charge settings, off by default (the owner, 2026-11-27): off, the fine tube's PID works on the reading alone, as main's loop, with no speed cap and no starved stop; the weight a turn is still learned, for the tuner.

**The PID's envelope:** the integral held while a channel runs at its least or most rate (anti-windup), and the fine channel's rate held under what the forecast can still take before the stop (the latter with the forecast on). The first build found it the difference between 35 to 66% and 85 to 90% good from bad starting gains. Part of the PID, not an algorithm beside it.

**The verdict** is read once the slowest lag and the settle time have passed after the stop; with the scale never calibrated, 0.8 s and 0.2 s, so 1 s as v1.21 (the owner, 2026-11-27).

**A lifted cup:** dropped from gate 3 (the owner, 2026-11-26). Between throws, today's check stays: a steady reading 10 below zero is the cup off.

**The auto-tuner**, after each throw, per powder: `new_pid = tuner(old_pid, charge_data)`.
- **Its data:** each throw's final weight less the target and the time of the fine tube's part of it, from the coarse tube's stop to the throw's (the owner, 2026-11-27, the review's T1; first built on the whole throw's time), in RAM: the window being thrown (25 throws) and the throws thrown on the kept gains (the latest 75); a restart begins afresh from the profile's gains, and so does a change of target (the review's T2).
- **Its judgement,** the owner's objective (2026-11-26): the tolerance first, then quicker. Against the kept gains' throws: a window whose median time, the fine tube's part, passes 8 s loses to one within it (first built as 10 s on the whole throw, which at 60 gr put back every slower, more accurate trial; the review's T1), and between two past it the quicker wins; while both shares within the tolerance are under half, the smaller mean miss wins (far from good the share has almost no signal); else the window whose share, counted as the unit's verdict counts it, is higher by half the two shares' noise, or no lower and quicker. Counted, not modelled from the throws' spread: on the A&D's 0.02 steps the percentiles snapped to the grid, and windows at 56% and 68% good both read as 49% (the bench, 2026-11-26, 23:10). Ratios only, so grams and grains are alike.
- **Its change,** the fine tube's kp and kd (the owner, 2026-11-26: "consider Kd as well"), by a factor, on two axes: the steady gain (kp moved, kd held) and the braking, 1 + kd x the weight a turn / 1000 (kd moved, kp with it so the steady gain holds; the D term is per millisecond, so the stored kd of 10 to 15 is a braking of 1.01, and the braking is never under 1). The braking slows the fine tube on what the forecast does not hold, the coarse tube's powder landing: a slower, narrower throw. Each window tries the kept gains moved one way (by 1.5 at first; the first way on an axis the one the throws miss by: over on average, a lower gain and more braking; my choice of 2026-11-26). A trial better than the kept gains (its judgement, above) is kept, and the same way tried again; a trial no better is put back and the other way tried at once, then the other axis; every way put back, nothing beats the kept gains and they are held, until their latest 50 throws pass less than they did by more than the noise, when the search begins again from them. **ki is never the tuner's** (the owner, 2026-11-26: "The tuner should never intentionally touch Ki. It user accidentially put some value in Ki, the tunner should compensate using Kp or Kd, never Ki."): the tuner has no ki, and an integral the user set is met by kp and kd. **Kd is the tuner's when it is on** (the owner, 2026-11-26: "If auto tuner is on, then Kd should be adjusted autonmously. The user should already grant the consent.").
- **Stored** into the powder's profile, the kept kp and kd only (a trial stays in RAM), as writes through the settings, so the web and the export show the gains it reached; at most one store a window.
- **The switch,** `auto_tune` in the charge settings, off by default (the owner, 2026-11-27): off, the profile is left as it is.
