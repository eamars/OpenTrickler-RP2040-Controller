# Development plans

This folder holds the firmware's architecture decision records (ADRs): what was decided, why, and plans not yet built. It is not the authority for current behaviour; that lives in the source.

This index says only where each ADR stands. What is known, what will be done and how the unit shows it works are in the ADR's folder; what was built and measured is in its `progress.md` once work starts.

Status: **Draft**, being discussed, not approved. **Decided**, approved and not built. **Open**, partly built, with the part under "Left" still to do. **Built**, built, flashed and proven on a unit, nothing left. **Archived**, removed from the tree.

How an ADR is written: in plain words. What we want, the owner's direction, what we know (measured, or checked in the code), what we will do and why, and how the unit shows it works. Work on an ADR starts when the owner approves it, and one ADR is open at a time. An ADR is closed (Built, in its README, its progress and this index) in its own branch before its PR is opened. A large ADR is staged by gates inside it, not split into small ADRs: a gate is approved and built on its own and may be merged by a PR of its own, the ADR then Open until its last gate (the owner, 2026-11-15). A functional change and a dependency update are separate ADRs. The process is the Open Weapon Mount Computer firmware's.

An ADR's number is its name and never changes. The table is in the order proposed for the work, which is not the order of the numbers: ADR-011 and ADR-012 were added on 2026-11-14 and come second and third; ADR-013 was added on 2026-11-15 and comes before ADR-012; ADR-014 was added the same day and comes after ADR-006. On 2026-11-17 the owner put the current Pico platform first: ADR-010, ADR-008, ADR-009 and ADR-014, then ADR-006 and ADR-007 together. ADR-015 was added the same day and proposed between ADR-010 and ADR-008, before the set-up wizard is built; on 2026-11-18 the owner folded it into ADR-008. On 2026-11-26 the owner moved ADR-006 before ADR-014: the earlier the split, the less there is to move later. ADR-006 now builds none of ADR-007, which stays after ADR-014. Each ADR says under "Why here" why it sits where it does. ADR-001 is written to be started as it stands. The others capture what is known and the ideas so far, and get their full text when their turn comes. ADR-016 was added on 2026-11-27, at ADR-009's merge, for what an AI agent needs of the API to help tune a powder; the owner has not placed it in the order yet.
